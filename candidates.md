## Why the selected candidate wins

Nothing in it announces sadness. It is built like a real status line — a label, a small fact, a quieter fact — and the grief lives entirely in one small specific thing: a goodnight that never sent, still sitting in the outbox, still true. The last clause does not plead or perform; it just leaves the message where it is, which is what makes it read as real grief rather than a performance of it. Lowercase throughout, plain words, no metaphor asking to be admired.
